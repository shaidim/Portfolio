What I Do

Bid Coordination From Enquiry to Submission

I support the parts of a tender that have to line up before a bid can go out: scope, quantities, supplier inputs, technical compliance, commercial pricing, documentation and revisions.

01

Tender & Bid Coordination

Review enquiry requirements, submission instructions, addenda and deadlines, then keep the package moving toward a complete submission.

Typical OutputTender Checklist · Submission Tracker · Revision Control
02

Commercial Estimating

Build quotations from supplier pricing, quantities, commercial assumptions, inclusions, exclusions, warranty and lead-time requirements.

Typical OutputBOQ · Pricing Schedule · Commercial Offer
03

BOQ & Quantity Validation

Check client-provided quantities and perform take-offs when required so pricing is based on a controlled scope.

Typical OutputBOQ Review · Quantity Check · Take-Off
04

Supplier & Manufacturer RFQs

Issue enquiries, follow up quotations and technical data, compare responses and coordinate outstanding information against bid deadlines.

Typical OutputRFQ Log · Supplier Tracker · Quotation Comparison
05

Technical & Commercial Comparison

Compare specifications, compliance, alternatives, lead times and commercial conditions so decision points are visible before submission.

Typical OutputBid Comparison · Alternative Offer
06

Submission & Revision Control

Assemble technical and commercial deliverables, manage corrigenda and clarifications, and update packages through revised or best-and-final offers.

Typical OutputDocument Register · Clarification Log · Final Submission Checklist

Software I Use to Move Bids Forward

The software changes by client and tender, but the workflow stays consistent: costing, quantity validation, plan review, document preparation, tender portals, coordination and file control.

Microsoft Excel

Primary costing tool for BOQ pricing, commercial build-ups, supplier comparisons, alternative offers, quotation summaries, trackers and tender registers.

PlanSwift

Used for quantity take-offs when a client BOQ is not provided and to recheck or validate quantities against drawings when required.

AutoCAD

Used to review plans and CAD drawings for scope understanding, coordination and quantity / layout checks.

Microsoft Word & PowerPoint

Used for documentary support, tender narratives, technical / commercial supporting documents and presentation materials.

Internal ERP used to support quotation, commercial and tender-related workflows within the company.

Outlook · Microsoft Teams · Zoom

Day-to-day coordination with clients, consultants, suppliers, manufacturers and internal teams across remote tender work.

About

Engineering Built the Foundation. Tendering Became the Specialty.

I am a Registered Civil Engineer whose work has developed from construction documentation and estimating into tender and bid coordination.

In a Singapore-based vendor environment, I coordinate enquiries, supplier and manufacturer inputs, BOQs, technical requirements, commercial quotations, tender documents and revisions across multiple concurrent bids.

My role is the coordination point between what the tender requires, what suppliers provide, and what the final submission must contain.

I have supported tender and quotation packages for projects in the Philippines, Singapore and other international markets across hospitality, residential, mixed-use, airport and integrated developments.

My specialization is getting technically clear, commercially controlled bids ready for submission.
